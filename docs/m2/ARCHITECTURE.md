# Offline routing engine

`tasks` → `complexity` → mandatory `eligibility` → `economics` → `ranking` → `engine.Decision`.

- `tasks.py`: 16 initial families; metadata-only multi-label classification; unknowns fail closed.
- `complexity.py`: deterministic v1 bands, reasons and missing-step uncertainty; long context is a resource signal rather than proof of reasoning difficulty. Financial analysis is high stakes independently of user criticality hints. Other risk classifications must be enriched by trusted upstream policy.
- `contracts.py`: proposed M1 immutable validated contracts and adapter Protocol; model/version binding, expiry, currency, finite decimals, identity uniqueness, 2,000-model cap.
- `eligibility.py`: approval, capabilities, modalities, context, outputs, region/residency, trusted privacy allow-list, restricted approval, evidence freshness/coverage/model binding, samples, Wilson quality, reliability and latency.
- `economics.py`: Decimal per-attempt charges with cached pricing; geometric bounded retry attempts for transport failures. Quality failures are terminal. All transport failures/timeouts charge the full estimated attempt; no failure is assumed free. Expected completion lower bound is `min(WilsonLower, 1-failure)*sum(failure**i)`; expected request charges are `attempt_charge*sum(failure**i)`. Cost per expected validated completion divides these quantities. Measured cost per success is independent observed total cost / successful tasks. Currency conversion is deliberately unavailable. Eight-decimal ceiling rounds estimates conservatively; the unrounded all-attempt bound is rounded once for budget eligibility. Ranges cover one through all allowed attempts; they are scenario bounds, not statistical confidence intervals.
- `policies.py`: explicit approved/enabled versions; tenant tightening and approved rollback. Quality-first uses Wilson lower bound then cost/latency; balanced chooses nondominated options then lowest expected charges; cost-efficient minimizes expected cost per completion; latency-sensitive minimizes p95; restricted raises quality to at least .95, samples to 500, reliability failure cap to .02 and requires restricted model class/approval. Platform and tenant versions are input to replay. No arbitrary weighted intelligence score.
- `ranking.py`: hard-qualified candidates only, Pareto quality/cost/latency and stable model-ID tie breaks. Bounded O(n²) Pareto pass; simulation additionally limits candidate-workload product to 200,000.
- `engine.py`: structured recommendations/no-route, privacy-safe records, explicit clock and correlation, canonical replay hashes, M1 boundary and fault handling. No HTTP endpoint or execution imports.
- `simulation.py`: bounded seeded paired workloads, fixed-model baseline and four policies, injected transport failure multiplier, actual simulated attempts charged even on failure, conditional objective success and synthetic latency. Financial budgets deplete only for simulated attempts after conservative eligibility. Fixed seed and fixture versions reproduce reports.
- `demo.py`: three invented models, prices and performance fixtures; CLI emits base and failure-sensitivity reports. No network imports or provider egress.

## Limits

No calibration against real models; quality intervals assume independent Bernoulli evidence. Family coverage is adapter-attested, not separate per-family statistical strata. Pricing covers token input/output/cache and full-attempt failure charges; non-token billing needs a new price schema, and such models must not be approved under v1. No measured p50/p99 distribution, public API, live reservation, storage service or automatic policy deployment. Simulations are synthetic estimates and cannot establish real savings. Legacy foundation behavior and disabled execution adapter remain unchanged.
